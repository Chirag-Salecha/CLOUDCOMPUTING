Performance Analysis

1. Response Time:
Response time increased significantly at higher concurrency.
It was 22.23 ms at W1 and reached 58.02 ms at W5.

2. Throughput:
Throughput increased from 44.83 req/s to a maximum of
289.25 req/s at 8 concurrent requests. It then decreased
to 257.70 req/s at 16 concurrent requests.

3. Failed Requests:
All 500 requests were successful. No failed requests were observed.

4. CPU:
CPU utilization remained between 0.01% and 0.02%.

5. Memory:
Attendance Service consumed the highest memory among the
three services.

6. Performance Degradation:
At 16 concurrent requests, response time increased and
throughput decreased compared with 8 concurrent requests.
